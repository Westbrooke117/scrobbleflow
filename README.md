An interactive data visualisation tool built to explore and analyse your Last.fm listening history. ScrobbleFlow uses your scrobbles to create interactive timelines and detailed charts, giving you a detailed view into your artist, album, and track listening history.

**Try it out at** https://westbrooke117.github.io/scrobbleflow/

## Key Features
- Compare your artist, album, or track data against each other with a variety of chart types
- Forecasting options let you see where your listening habits are trending
- Search through all of your scrobbled items for comparison
- Data smoothing lets find trends without spikes in data
- Export your chart as either an image or to a CSV file
